Lakewood, CO
Residence

This two-story home was designed for a growing family in Lakewood, Colorado. The space plan balances an open layout with the family’s work, entertaining, hobby, storage needs and future plans in mind. Flexible and functional spaces were incorporated to accommodate both their current lifestyle and changing needs.

First floor

The first floor centers on an open kitchen, dining, and living area designed for everyday use and entertaining. The kitchen accommodates two cooks and island seating, while adjacent spaces provide dedicated areas for sewing and yoga, laundry and mudroom functions, pet needs, and storage for outdoor gear and hobby supplies.

Second floor

The second floor was designed for privacy and to adapt with the family’s needs over time. The primary suite includes a spacious bedroom, walk-in closet, and bath with a large freestanding tub, while the guest bedroom can transition into a child’s room. A separate home office incorporates a Murphy bed, allowing the space to also accommodate overnight guests.
