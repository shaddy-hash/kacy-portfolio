About me

I’m an Interior Design student at ACC with more than 20 years of professional experience in art direction, graphic design, and advertising.

My design background has taught me to consider each project as a whole without losing sight of the details. I approach interior design by bringing together research, spatial thinking, materials, visual communication, and technical requirements to create thoughtful environments for the people who use them.
